# KalaSetu

### Discover handmade. Connect with the maker.

KalaSetu is a digital marketplace that helps small artisans showcase and sell their handmade products directly to buyers.

## 🚀 Features

- 🛍️ Browse handmade products
- 🔎 Search and filter products
- 🎨 Explore craft categories
- 👨‍🎨 Meet the Maker — discover artisan stories
- 💬 Direct communication with artisans
- 🛒 Checkout and place orders
- 📦 Order management
- ➕ Artisan product listing
- 👤 Buyer & Artisan profiles

## 📱 Installation

### Install the Android APK

1. Open the **Releases** section of this repository.
2. Open the latest release.
3. Download `app-release.apk`.
4. Transfer the APK to your Android phone if necessary.
5. Open the APK.
6. Allow installation from unknown sources if Android asks.
7. Tap **Install**.
8. Open **KalaSetu**.

The latest APK is available under:

**Releases → KalaSetu v1.0.0 → app-release.apk**

💡 Problem Being Solved

Small artisans often struggle to reach customers and showcase their handmade products beyond their local markets.

KalaSetu provides a simple digital marketplace where artisans can present their products while buyers can discover handmade crafts and connect directly with the people who create them.
⭐ Key Differentiator
Meet the Maker

Instead of treating handmade products as anonymous marketplace listings, KalaSetu highlights the story behind the product and the artisan who created it.

This creates a more personal connection between buyers and makers while helping promote traditional craftsmanship.
